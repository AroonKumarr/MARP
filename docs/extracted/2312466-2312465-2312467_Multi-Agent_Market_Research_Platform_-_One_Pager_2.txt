Purpose
Businesses and researchers need reliable findings without spending so much time gath-
ering and checking the same information. The proposed system brings research work
together to reduce repetitive tasks and help teams assess their evidence. Its purpose is
to make market research easier to manage and business decisions easier to justify.
Introduction
Market research teams often work with survey files, field records and online sources
that sit in separate places. Researchers spend time matching and checking these records
before analysis can begin. The proposed system uses cooperating AI agents to reduce
this manual work and help teams explain the evidence behind their findings.
Targeted Audience
The main users are market researchers, data analysts and business strategists who
compare customer feedback and campaign results. These users need a clear link between
findings and evidence. Business owners and company managers form the secondary au-
dience, particularly when they conduct research without a dedicated analysis team.
Product Functions and Features
The system uses AI agents to plan research, collect web information and extract
relevant details. Researchers examine survey responses alongside secondary sources. The
system cleans data and compares sources to identify gaps or conflicting information for
review. Users compare competitors and results across different periods to understand
market changes. Interactive dashboards present these comparisons, and users adjust
the views through natural language commands. Users ask the conversational assistant
questions about the research data and receive answers with links to supporting evidence.
Human reviewers assess uncertain records before those records enter the analysis.
Social and Industrial Impact
For research teams, the main gain will be time. They will no longer match records
manually. Their effort will instead go into interpreting findings. Smaller businesses and
independent researchers will use analysis tools without hiring a large team. Consumers
will give feedback through online questionnaires or field interviews. The interviews will
reach places with limited internet access.
Companies will turn the findings into campaign plans and decide where to put their
research budgets. Researchers will back up their conclusions with supporting records.
This will let others check the work and make research more accountable.
